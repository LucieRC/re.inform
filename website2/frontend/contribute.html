Example submission

Extremist narrative:

"Immigrants are stealing our jobs and draining public resources"

Counter-argument:

Studies consistently show immigrants create jobs through entrepreneurship and fill critical labor gaps in sectors like healthcare, agriculture, and technology. Research indicates they contribute more in taxes than they receive in benefits, with a net positive economic impact. For example, a 2018 study by the National Academies found that immigrant-founded companies create jobs for millions of native-born workers.
