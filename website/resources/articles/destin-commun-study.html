The "Destin Commun" Typology: A Framework for Tailored Communication

By re.inform Team | April 2023
Personality Types Research French Context

Understanding the psychological profiles that make people susceptible to extremist content is essential for developing effective counter-narratives. The "Destin Commun" study provides a valuable framework for categorizing different audience segments in the French context, allowing for more targeted and effective communication strategies.

About the Study

The survey was conducted by Destin Commun (the French branch of the international network More in Common). The report was authored under the direction of François-Xavier Demoures, with contributions from Lucie Monges, Maïder Piola, Anaïz Parfait, and Mathieu Lefèvre, and carried out in partnership with the Kantar institute.

The study is one of the most comprehensive examinations of the French population's beliefs and values, using psychological profiling rather than traditional demographic categories.

Visit the original source →

Classification Principles

Its methodology relies on:

  • The segmentation is based exclusively on respondents' answers to questions about their core beliefs and level of civic engagement — not on sociodemographic categories.
  • The authors used hierarchical ascending classification (typological analysis) and examined six dimensions from social psychology: group belongings, threat perception, optimism/pessimism, parenting style / authoritarian personality, moral foundations, and agency/responsibility.
  • The survey involved more than 6,000 respondents (data collected throughout 2019).

Usefulness for Adapting Online Discussions

Because membership in these "families" is highly predictive of opinions on a wide range of topics, this typology is useful for designing targeted messages and debate formats:

  • Tonality (calming vs. combative)
  • Mobilization angles (social justice, security, economic future)
  • Levels of information or action solicited depending on the target group

In practice, it can help segment audiences and test narratives aimed at reducing polarization or promoting listening.

Limitations

While valuable, this framework has several limitations for our specific purpose:

  1. Temporality / timeliness: Data collected in 2019 — distributions and opinions may have evolved since then.
  2. Absence of behavioral variables: The typology is based on declared beliefs, not on actual online engagement data (clicks, shares, influencers), which limits operational precision for behavioral micro-targeting.
  3. Ethical risks and perverse effects: Adapting debates to groups can improve reception, but also reinforce echo chambers, emotionally manipulate vulnerable audiences, or stigmatize categories.
  4. Simplification: Six "families" synthesize belief systems but do not account for intra-group diversity or local contextual situations.
  5. Survey methodology: All quotas/surveys have margins of error and non-response bias; operational use requires continuous validation and cross-referencing with behavioral data.

Applying This Framework to Counter-Narratives

Despite its limitations, the Destin Commun typology offers valuable insights for our work at re.inform:

  • Targeting messages: Understanding which core beliefs drive susceptibility to extremist content allows us to craft more effective counter-narratives
  • Identifying engagement strategies: Different "families" respond to different communication styles and value appeals
  • Testing approaches: The typology provides a framework for A/B testing different messaging strategies with specific audience segments

Conclusion

The Destin Commun typology is a solid empirical tool for developing communication and debate strategies (tone, topics, levers) because it maps "deep convictions" that predict opinions. However, its operational use online must be cautious: updates, cross-referencing with behavioral data, and ethical safeguards are essential.
