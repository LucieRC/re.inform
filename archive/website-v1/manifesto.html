Our Manifesto

The vision, values, and approach that guide our work

Our Vision

We envision a world where extremist ideologies lose their power through improved dialogue and understanding. re.inform exists to build bridges between individuals by developing evidence-based communication strategies tailored to different personality types.

We believe that many individuals who adopt extremist viewpoints do so because traditional counter-arguments fail to address their underlying psychological needs and worldviews. By understanding these foundations better, we can develop more effective engagement strategies.

Our Values

Evidence-Based Approaches

We ground our work in rigorous research from psychology, sociology, and communication studies.

Inclusivity

We recognize the diversity of human experience and avoid one-size-fits-all solutions.

Bridge-Building

We focus on creating connections rather than deepening divides.

Collaborative Learning

We believe in the collective wisdom of diverse contributors and continuous improvement.

Our Approach

Personality Categorization

Our approach begins by understanding that people adopt extremist viewpoints for different reasons, based on their personalities, needs, and contexts. We develop these categorizations through:

  • Integration of existing personality frameworks
  • Collaborations with leading researchers in social sciences
  • Digital analysis of online communities and their conceptual frameworks
  • Continuous refinement through community feedback

Counter-Narrative Development

For each identified personality type, we develop tailored communication strategies that:

  • Address underlying needs and concerns
  • Use language and framing that resonates
  • Introduce alternative perspectives in accessible ways
  • Encourage critical thinking without triggering defensive reactions

Expected Impact

We envision our tools being used by:

  • Activists engaging with communities vulnerable to extremist recruitment
  • Educators developing more effective critical thinking curricula
  • Journalists crafting reporting that doesn't inadvertently reinforce extremist narratives
  • Friends and family trying to maintain relationships with loved ones who have adopted concerning viewpoints
  • Political leaders seeking to communicate more effectively with diverse constituencies

Through these applications, we aim to reduce the appeal and spread of harmful ideologies by making counter-narratives more psychologically accessible and effective.
